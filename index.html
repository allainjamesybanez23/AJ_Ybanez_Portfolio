AEye

Undergraduate thesis

  • Team of 3
  • July 2026 - present

A Windows desktop app that proctors computer-based exams. It reads eye gaze, head pose and posture from two cameras, compares them to each student's own baseline, and sends real-time alerts to a human proctor, who makes the final call.

My part

  • Side-camera module. Worked on upper-body posture and hand tracking with MediaPipe, and phone and extra-person detection with YOLOv8.
  • Per-student calibration. Worked on mapping each student's screen area with a convex hull (Graham scan).
  • Evidence logging. Worked on logging each confirmed incident with start and end times and screenshots, plus a live gaze and head-pose graph.
  • Python
  • PySide6
  • OpenCV
  • MediaPipe
  • YOLOv8
  • scikit-learn
  • MySQL

Haircut Policy Detector

Real-time computer vision

  • Personal project
  • May 2026

A real-time camera app that checks students' haircuts against the USJ-R grooming policy, the kind of check a guard does at the school gate.

What it does

  • Detects faces and eyes with OpenCV Haar cascades, and classifies gender with an SVM trained on HOG features from a dataset I collected, so the right rules are applied.
  • Segments the hair region to flag bangs that fall too low on the forehead and hair that is too tall, with a separate side-profile check.
  • A Tkinter dashboard shows the live feed, a Compliant or Violation status, the gender confidence and a debug mask of the detected hair.
  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • scikit-learn
  • NumPy
  • Tkinter

RFID Door Lock

IoT door lock

  • Team project
  • 2024

A door lock that opens with a registered RFID card or from a web page, built on an ESP32 and installed on a real door.

My part: Worked on the code, set up the MQTT connection between the ESP32 and the web page, and configured the door hardware.

What it does

  • An ESP32 reads RFID cards, drives the lock and shows the status on an LCD.
  • A Node.js and Express web page locks or unlocks the door and registers new cards, talking to the ESP32 over MQTT.
  • The page shows live lock events and invalid-card alerts through Server-Sent Events.
  • ESP32
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • MQTT

Save the Fuel

Hackathon app

  • Team project
  • April 2026

2nd Runner-Up, USJ-R School of Computer Studies Annual Hackathon

An offline-first app that helps Cebu jeepney drivers save fuel by catching engine idling in traffic.

What it does

  • Detects idling from live GPS speed and the phone's motion sensor inside mapped Cebu traffic hotspots.
  • Color-coded screen glows and Cebuano voice cues ("Cut engine, manoy!") tell drivers when to turn off the engine.
  • Tracks pesos saved, compares fuel prices by station, and projects take-home pay, all stored offline.
  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • SQLite
